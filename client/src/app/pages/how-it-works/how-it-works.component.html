How SmartPaint Works

A manual, transparent workflow — no guesswork about what the tool is doing to your photo.

STEP 01

Upload Your Room

Upload a clear, well-lit JPG or PNG photo of the room. Straight-on shots of a single wall give the most convincing preview.

STEP 02

Select Your Wall

Mark the wall using the brush or polygon tool. Undo, redo and erase are available while you refine the selection.

STEP 03

Choose Your Color

Pick from the curated library. Colors are applied as a translucent overlay so the room's shadows and texture stay visible.

STEP 04

Adjust Opacity & Finish

Fine-tune opacity and brightness, then switch between matte, satin and glossy finishes to see how light behaves.

STEP 05

Compare Before & After

Use the comparison slider to see the original wall next to the painted result before you commit.

STEP 06

Save & Download

Save the design to your projects, then download a PNG to share with your family, painter or designer.
